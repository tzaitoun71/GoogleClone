Data Science

Data science is the field of extracting insights from data. It combines statistics, programming, and domain knowledge to answer questions and make predictions from large amounts of information.

Tools of the trade

Python is the most common language in data science because of its rich ecosystem of libraries for analyzing data, building charts, and training machine learning models.

Where the data lives

Before data can be analyzed it has to be collected and stored. Much of it lives in databases, which data scientists query to pull the records they need.
